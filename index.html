Story: The Night Of The Theft

There is a jewelry shop, and Mr. Mehra is the owner. He is sad because last night someone stole his jewelry.

Now the market is full of rumors, and everyone is under suspicion. You need to inspect clues, question suspects, and catch the thief before time runs out.

Mr. Mehra: "Please help me find who stole the diamond before time runs out."

CASE INTRO

This is a 6-character story including Mr. Mehra and 5 suspects, out of which one is the thief.

menu_book Game Rules & How To Play

Please read the rules and check all agreement boxes before starting the game.

Rules

  • You have only 5 minutes total.
  • You have only 3 final guesses.
  • Find the thief before attempts or time ends.

Stages

  • Stage 1 (Suspects): You will be given 6 character cards. Use Interrogate to reveal details like jacket color, fingerprint match, and late entry info.
  • Stage 2 (Clues): You will be given clue cards from witnesses and evidence. Analyze every clue to understand who matches all conditions.
  • Stage 3 (Guess): You need to choose one suspect as your culprit guess. You have only 3 total attempts, so each guess matters.
  • Stage 4 (Result): The game shows whether your guess is correct. You win if you catch the thief within 5 minutes and within 3 attempts.
